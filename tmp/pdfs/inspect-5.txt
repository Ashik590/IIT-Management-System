7.1 State Transition Diagram
A state transition diagram represents the active states of a class and the events (triggers) that move it from
one state to another. Based on the process description and the CRC model of the Intern and Placement
Office System (IPOS), all events, their initiators, and their collaborators were identified for the eleven classes
of the system: Student, IPO Head, IPOC Member 1, IPOC Member 2, IPOC Member 3, Company, Intern
Position, Application, Allocation, Letter, and Regular Program Officer.
