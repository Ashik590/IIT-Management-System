1. Problem Statement Summary
The Intern and Placement Office (IPO) of XYZ Institute manages the annual internship placement of BSSE 4th
Year students. The internship semester runs from 1 January to 30 June each year.
Interested software companies send their willingness to take interns to the IPO Head. IPOC Member 1 records
company information, available job positions, responsibilities, company address and HR email address. IPOC
Member 2 informs students to submit their company/job choices in priority order. Students also upload a CV
and submit skill information with competency rank, where rank 1 is the highest competency.
IPOC Member 3 records each student's CGPA up to Third Year First Semester. Allocation considers CGPA
order, the student's preferences, skill match and position availability. The IPO Head may manually place a
student in any company.
After allocation, a formal selection letter is generated for company HR and sent by email on behalf of the IPO
Head. A copy is sent to the Regular Program Officer for office record. Companies issue offer letters to selected
interns and send a copy to the IPO Head.


2. Modeling Rules Used
    GC: a noun is a potential class if it meets at least 3 of 7 General Characteristics, or if it is GC1 (External
     Entity).
    SC: if a potential class satisfies SC6 (Essential Requirements), it is selected as a class.
    GC is a gate: nouns that fail GC do not proceed to Selection Criteria.
    Final analysis class set: IPO Personnel, Student, Company, Student Preference, Job Position, Skill,
     Allocation, Email.
