Modelling rule. The early stages remain intentionally broad. A noun that passes GC is only a candidate.
Similar GC candidates are merged before SC. A merged candidate becomes a selected class when it
satisfies at least three SC points or the Essential Requirement criterion. Finalization then confirms
inheritance, responsibility placement and collaboration.

2. General Classifications (GC)
The extracted nouns are examined through seven General Classifications. These categories are used as
non-exclusive analytical lenses: one noun may satisfy several classifications. A noun passes GC when it
satisfies at least three classifications, or when it satisfies External Entity by itself.

                                         Table 2: Seven General Classifications

 No.      General Classification            Description

 1        External entities                 People, organizations, devices or external services that exchange information with the
                                            system.

 2        Things                            Physical or informational objects that the system stores, manages or manipulates.

 3        Events                            Occurrences, transactions or lifecycle changes that the system must recognize or
                                            record.

 4        Roles                             Capacities performed by people or software actors.

 5        Organizational units              Groups or functional management units with coordinated responsibilities.

 6        Places                            Physical or logical locations relevant to system interaction.

 7        Structures                        Composite records, aggregates, hierarchies or organized information structures.

3.2 Applying the Six Selection Criteria
The merged potential classes are evaluated using six criteria. A candidate passes when it satisfies at least
three criteria, or when Essential Requirement is accepted. Essential Requirement means that the
requirement needs an independent class, not merely that the associated feature must exist.

                                                      Table 5: Six Selection Criteria

 No.      Selection Criterion                      Description

 1        Retained Information                     Information about the candidate must be remembered by the system.

 6        Essential Requirement           An independent class is essential to satisfy the requirement.

7. Finalizing Classes
The selected classes are finalized by checking responsibility placement, inheritance opportunities and
unnecessary duplication. The following decisions produce the final high-level class model.

1. Authentication separation. Account credentials, status and permission behaviour are centralized in
UserAccount. Member, YogaInstructor and ExecutiveCommitteeMember collaborate with it instead of
duplicating authentication data.

2. Membership lifecycle. Applicant, MembershipRegistration and MembershipApplication are not retained
as separate classes. Member represents both pending and active membership states. The static method
Member.registerForMembership(...) creates a pending member and stores the submitted information.

3. Verification rule. The Treasurer receives the application and an ExecutiveCommitteeMember verifies its
information. There is no separate formal approval operation. Membership becomes active only after
verification and successful registration payment.

4. Committee hierarchy. ExecutiveCommitteeMember is a concrete superclass representing general
executive members. President, Secretary, Treasurer, PublicitySecretary, CulturalSecretary and
FieldEquipmentSecretary specialize it because they have distinct permissions and methods.

5. Activity hierarchy. Activity is abstract and contains shared schedule, organizer, participant and
attendance behaviour. YogaSession and Event are its concrete subclasses.

6. Attendance placement. Attendance and AttendanceRecord are absorbed into Activity as
attendanceEntries and attendance operations. A separate Attendance class would duplicate the activity
context.

7. Event participation. EventParticipation and EventRegistration        are   absorbed   into   Event   as
registeredMembers, participationInformation and registration methods.

8. Financial consolidation. FinancialTransaction, payment, donation, income, expense and reports are
maintained by FinancialManagement. PaymentGateway remains separate because it represents the
external payment boundary.

9. Communication consolidation. Notice, announcement, activity post and event media share publication
attributes and operations and are therefore merged into MediaPost.

10. Yoga knowledge consolidation. Pose information, instructions, risks, precautions and videos are
consolidated in YogaContent. VajraAssistant remains separate because it provides conversational retrieval
and safety guidance.

11. Inventory consolidation. Equipment, assignments, returns, operational tasks and helping-hand
assignee information are maintained by InventoryManagement. Task and HelpingHand are not
independent classes in this high-level model.

12. Presentation and automation. MemberDashboard, SystemAppearance and general SystemAutomation
are boundary or presentation behaviours. They do not represent independent domain classes.
