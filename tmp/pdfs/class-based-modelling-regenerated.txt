 Modelling Rule
 The total-noun list contains noun phrases explicitly extracted from the usage scenario. The
 solution-space list is a subset containing nouns represented or used by the proposed system. A
 solution-space noun passes General Classification when it satisfies at least three classifications or
 satisfies External Entity by itself. Only GC-passed nouns are evaluated by the Selection Criteria. A GC
 candidate passes SC when it satisfies at least three criteria or satisfies Essential Requirement. No
 separate merge stage is used; accepted source nouns are standardized into class names during
 finalization.

 3.1 Seven General Classifications

  1      External entities                 People, organizations, devices, or external services that exchange information with
                                           the system.

  2      Things                            Physical or informational objects that the system stores, manages, or manipulates.

  3      Events                            Occurrences, transactions, or lifecycle changes that the system must recognize or
                                           record.

  4      Roles                             Capacities performed by people or software actors.

  5      Organizational units              Groups or functional management units with coordinated responsibilities.

  6      Places                            Physical or logical locations relevant to system interaction.

  7      Structures                        Composite records, aggregates, hierarchies, or organized information structures.

 4. Selection Criteria (SC)
 Only the 51 GC-passed nouns are evaluated. A candidate passes when it satisfies at least three criteria
 or satisfies Essential Requirement. Nineteen candidates pass and are standardized into the final class
 names; thirty-two fail as independent classes but may still contribute attributes, records, or behaviour
 to a selected class.


 4.1 Six Selection Criteria
  No.     Selection Criterion            Description

  1       Retained Information           Information about the candidate must be remembered by the system.

  2       Needed Services                The candidate provides identifiable services required by the system.

  3       Multiple Attributes            The candidate needs several meaningful attributes.

  4       Common Attributes              Instances or related subclasses share attributes.

  5       Common Operations              Instances or related subclasses share operations.

  6       Essential Requirement          An independent class is essential to satisfy the requirement.

 8. Finalizing Classes
 The selected classes are finalized by checking responsibility placement, inheritance opportunities, and
 unnecessary duplication.

  1. Authentication separation. Account credentials, status, and permission behaviour are centralized in
     UserAccount. Member, YogaInstructor, and ExecutiveCommitteeMember collaborate with it
     instead of duplicating authentication data.

  2. Membership lifecycle. Applicant, MembershipRegistration, and MembershipApplication are not
     retained as separate classes. Member represents both pending and active membership states. The
     public Member.registerForMembership(...) operation creates the pending member record
     without requiring an account or login. The model has no separate submission, correction, or
     application-status-viewing operation.

  3. Verification and activation rule. An ExecutiveCommitteeMember views the pending membership
     application and either verifies its information or dismisses it. Incorrect information is dismissed
     rather than returned for correction. Registration payment occurs only after verification.
     PaymentGateway confirms online payment automatically, while the Treasurer records an accepted
     offline payment. Once verification and payment are both recorded, the system invokes
     activateMembership(), generateMembershipNumber(), generateQRCode(), and
     issueMembershipCard() on the pending Member record. There is no separate formal approval
     operation.

  4. Committee hierarchy. ExecutiveCommitteeMember is a concrete superclass representing general
     executive members. President, Secretary, Treasurer, PublicitySecretary,
     CulturalSecretary, and FieldEquipmentSecretary specialize it because they have distinct
     permissions and methods.

  5. Activity hierarchy. Activity is abstract and contains shared schedule, organizer, participant, and
     attendance behaviour. YogaSession and Event are its concrete subclasses.

  6. Attendance placement. Attendance and AttendanceRecord are absorbed into Activity as
     attendanceEntries and attendance operations. A separate Attendance class would duplicate the
     activity context.

  7. Event participation. EventParticipation and EventRegistration are absorbed into Event as
     registeredMembers, participationInformation, and registration methods.

  8. Financial consolidation. FinancialTransaction, payment, donation, income, expense, and reports
     are maintained by FinancialManagement. PaymentGateway remains separate because it represents
     the external payment boundary.

  9. Communication consolidation. Notice, announcement, activity post, and event media share
     publication attributes and operations and are merged into MediaPost. Notification is separate: it
     handles direct attendance, milestone, and event messages, while MediaPost represents published
     content.

  10. Yoga knowledge consolidation. Pose information, instructions, risks, precautions, and videos are
     consolidated in YogaContent. VajraAssistant remains separate because it provides conversational
     retrieval and safety guidance.

  11. Equipment responsibility. Equipment catalogue, availability, assignment, and return information
    are owned directly by FieldEquipmentSecretary; a separate inventory-management class would
     duplicate that role. Operational tasks, task tracking, and HelpingHand are not retained in this
     high-level model.

Class-Based Modelling - Ramna Yoga Society Management System




  12. Presentation and automation. MemberDashboard, SystemAppearance, and general
    SystemAutomation are boundary or presentation behaviours rather than independent domain
     classes.

  13. Role-specific workflow. The CulturalSecretary manages events, submits event financial details
    to the Treasurer, and requests event publicity. The Treasurer records the resulting financial
    entries, while the President reviews overdue members and takes organizational action when
    required.

  14. Notification responsibility. Notification selects Email or SMS, composes the message, and sends
    it for each member's session check-in, an attendance milestone, a newly created event, or a
    completed event registration. A new-event announcement is sent to all members. Attendance
    messages go only to members who checked in; absent members are not notified. The milestone is
    described at a high level without fixing a numeric threshold. Member supplies recipient contact
    information, while Activity, YogaSession, and Event provide the triggering context. Email and
    SMS remain delivery channels, not separate classes or CRC collaborators.
